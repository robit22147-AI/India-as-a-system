Human review required

AI supports analysis, not allegations.

  • Analysis: identifies possible system relationships.
  • Evidence: should be reviewed against verified sources.
  • Source: must be recorded by an authorised reviewer.
  • Verification state: AI output remains illustrative until reviewed.
  • Recommended action: human/admin review before any public alert.

Help & Support

Understand the system, at your own pace.

Support placeholder:  support@indiaasasystem.example
How does the system work?

It organizes example public-service themes across administrative levels, then shows how connected systems may influence one another.

How do I navigate System Scale?

Select Village, District, State, National or Global. The example focus areas update below the selected scale card.

Are the figures and articles real?

No. All articles, feedback, metrics and case-study figures are prominently labelled as sample or demo content.

How do I use What-If scenarios?

Choose one scenario to reveal an illustrative pathway across connected systems. These pathways are examples, not forecasts.

How does AI analysis work in this demo?

Demo AI responses illustrate system relationships only. Connect an approved API and verified sources before using live analysis.

How are public alerts verified?

Verified information is reviewed by a human/admin before it can be marked VERIFIED or PUBLISHED. AI cannot independently establish wrongdoing.

How can I report an issue?

Use the support placeholder for this demonstration. A production service should provide an approved reporting channel and evidence-handling process.

Where can I get support?
