Understand the system, at your own pace.

How does the system work?

It organizes example public-service themes across administrative levels, then shows how connected systems may influence one another.

How do I navigate System Scale?

Select Village, District, State, National or Global. The example focus areas update below the selected scale card.

Are the figures and articles real?

No. All articles, feedback, metrics and case-study figures are prominently labelled as sample or demo content.
